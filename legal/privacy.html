Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

What we collect

Account: phone number, display name, an ID, your public encryption key, your about text and profile photo, and a hashed login proof (not your password). Activity: last-seen time (you can hide it), and which accounts you chat with. Technical: error reports and basic request logs to keep the service working. Push: a notification token if you turn notifications on.

What we cannot see

Message text, photos, videos and voice notes are encrypted on your phone before upload. We store only encrypted data, which expires after about 24 hours. Your private key is stored encrypted and only your password can unlock it.

Lynko AI and Help center

These are the exception: what you type is sent to an AI provider (Groq) to produce an answer, and is not end-to-end encrypted. Don't share secrets there. AI history stays on your phone for 24 hours.

Contacts and nicknames

Names you give your contacts are saved only on your own device. They are not uploaded and the other person cannot see them.

Service providers

We use hosting, a database, object storage for encrypted media, a realtime messaging service (Pusher), Cloudflare (bot protection) and the AI provider above. They process data only to run Lynko.

Retention and deletion

Messages and media expire automatically. Account data stays until the account is deleted. To request deletion, ask in Settings → Help center.

Security

We use encryption and rate limits, but no system is perfectly secure. Use a strong password and keep your phone locked.

Children

Lynko is not intended for children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above.
